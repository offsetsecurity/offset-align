# What each screen is for

| Screen | What it is for |
|---|---|
| **Get ready** | The plan: what to do next, and how far you have got |
| **Golden thread** | Every risk, what treats it, and the proof, joined up. Broken links are what auditors find |
| **Dashboard** | Where you stand today, by function, in numbers |
| **Profile & Tiers** | Your current and target Tiers, and how far each function's profiles are filled in |
| **Subcategories** | All 106 outcomes in 6 functions: status, owner, priority, and your current and target profile |
| **Evidence** | The proof, with dates and which outcomes each item supports |
| **Risks** | What could go wrong, scored, owned and treated |
| **Assets** | What you hold that matters, and who owns it |
| **Policies** | Your scope, and your documents with their versions and review dates |
| **Tasks** | The work outstanding, with owners and dates |
| **Incidents** | What went wrong, how bad, and where it stands |
| **Findings** | What reviews and assessments found, and what closed it |
| **Reports** | PDFs: executive summary, gap report, readiness plan, risk and evidence registers |
| **Suppliers** | Who you rely on, what they hold, and what assurance they gave (GV.SC, supply chain). Has a sample library |
| **Training** | Who was trained, on what, and when it is due again (PR.AT, awareness and training) |
| **Objectives** | What you are aiming at, how it is measured, and whether you got there |
| **Interested parties** | Who has a stake in your security and what they need (GV.OC, organisational context) |
| **Audits & reviews** | Assessments and leadership reviews, planned and held (ID.IM, improvement) |
| **Communications** | What you tell people about security, to whom, how often, and when it is next due |
| **Escalation** | Automatic reminders for anything with a date and an owner's email, one email each, and who is told as it goes overdue |
| **Calendar** | Everything with a date on it, in one list |
| **Help** | Short answers, like this page |
| **Documentation** | The longer guides |
| **Users** | Who can sign in, and what they are allowed to do |
| **Audit trail** | Who did what, when, and from where. Read only. Administrators and auditors |
| **Backups** | Take one, download one, restore one |
| **Settings** | Email, the daily digest, updates, and the HTTPS certificate |

## Two things that surprise people

**A Tier is not a score.** Tiers describe how deliberately you manage
cybersecurity risk. CSF 2.0 is clear that a higher Tier is not automatically
better. Pick the one that fits the risk you carry, and say why.

**The profile boxes matter more than the status.** "Implemented" tells a reader
little. "Backups run nightly; nobody has tested a restore" tells them
everything, and it is what the target is measured against.
