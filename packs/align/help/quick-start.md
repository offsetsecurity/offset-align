# Quick start

Fifteen minutes. By the end you will know what the product holds, and what to
do tomorrow.

## Sign in

Your administrator gives you a username, a password and the address. The first
person ever to open a new install creates the administrator account.

## Look at Get ready

Seven stages, 24 steps, from setting the product up to a current and target
profile you are acting on. Each step says what to do, why it matters, and has a
button to the screen where you do it.

Two kinds of step:

- **Checked for you.** The product looks at its own data and turns them green.
- **Ticked by you.** Things no software can see, such as whether the budget
  holder agreed your target Tier.

## Write the scope

**Policies**, the box at the top. A paragraph on what this programme covers is
enough to start.

## Set your Tiers

**Profile & Tiers.** Where governance and risk management are today, and where
you intend them to be. Four choices, one to four. It takes five minutes and
frames everything after it.

## Put something in two registers

- **Assets:** open the **Sample library** tab and add the ten that look like
  yours. There are 132 to choose from.
- **Risks:** the same. There are 75 to react to, each already pointing at the
  outcomes that deal with it.

## Describe ten outcomes

Open **Subcategories** and pick ten you know well. For each, set a status and
write one sentence in the **Current profile** box: what actually happens today.

## Attach one piece of proof

**Evidence → Add.** Attach a file, date it, and link it to a subcategory. That
is the loop you will repeat: describe, improve, prove.

## Tomorrow

Export the **Gap report** from **Reports**. It lists every subcategory in scope
that is not yet implemented, with its priority. The ones marked High are your
first week's work.
