# Common questions

**Do I have to do the stages in order?**
No. Get ready suggests an order most organisations find easiest. Do anything you
can already do.

**A step will not tick.**
Steps with "The product checks this one itself" go green from your data. The
line underneath says what is missing, such as "105 of 106 subcategories
describe where you are now". Steps only a person can confirm are ticked by
hand.

**Something does not apply to us.**
Every step can be excluded with a reason, and so can every subcategory.
Excluded things stop counting against you, and stop needing a profile. Be
strict: "we write no software" is a reason, "we have not got round to it" is
not.

**Which Tier should we aim for?**
Most organisations belong at Tier 2 or 3. Tier 4 means you adapt from every
event and share what you learn, which is a lot of work to sustain. Choose a
target for governance and for risk management separately, and get it agreed
by whoever pays for it.

**What goes in the current and target profile boxes?**
A sentence each. Current: what actually happens today. Target: what good
enough looks like for you. "As now" is a legitimate target.

**How fresh does evidence have to be?**
The product flags anything older than 90 days. Set a collected date on
everything.

**Is there a CSF certificate?**
No. The Framework is voluntary and nobody certifies against it. What you get is
a clear, defensible picture of where you stand and where you are going, which
is what customers, insurers and boards ask for.

**We are a small team. Is this overkill?**
Use Get ready and the sample libraries, and mark what genuinely does not apply.
The Framework is written to scale down.

**Why does the browser say "Not secure"?**
The server has no certificate the browser trusts. An administrator fixes it
for everyone under **Settings → HTTPS certificate**, with a certificate from
your IT team. It is free: you do not need to buy one.
