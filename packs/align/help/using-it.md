# Using it day to day

The Framework assumes you get better over time. A profile written once and
never touched describes a company that no longer exists.

## Every day: the digest

Turn on the **daily digest** in Settings. One email lists what needs
attention: evidence out of date, overdue tasks and findings, policies due for
review, and the risks in the top band. If it is empty, nothing is slipping.

## Once a week: the tasks

Open **Tasks**. Close what is done, and change the date on anything that has
slipped, with a reason. Tasks with names and dates are what move you from the
current profile to the target.

## When something changes: the profile

Changed a process, bought a tool, closed a gap? Open the subcategory, update
its status and the **Current profile** sentence, and attach the new proof.

## When something goes wrong: the incident

**Incidents.** What happened, how bad, where it stands. Afterwards, note what
you learned and what you changed. RESPOND and RECOVER are judged on exactly
this record.

## When something is found: the finding

**Findings.** A review, a customer assessment or a penetration test found
something. Record what, the root cause, what you changed, and how you checked
it worked.

## Every quarter: evidence, risks and suppliers

Evidence older than 90 days is flagged. Collect it again, or change what you
are claiming. Look at the top of the risk register, and at the suppliers you
depend on most (GV.SC), and ask whether either has changed.

## Every quarter: the profile review

Walk the high-priority subcategories. Has the current profile moved towards
the target? Export the **Executive summary** and take it to whoever oversees
the programme. GV.OV asks for exactly that oversight, and this is the record
of it.

## Once a year: the Tiers

Look again at your current and target Tiers. If the business, its risks or its
regulators have changed, the target may need to change too.
