# Offset Align

**For the NIST Cybersecurity Framework 2.0.** Say where you are, decide where
you want to be, and close the distance. On your own server.

## Who it is for

Organisations of any size and sector using CSF 2.0 to run, measure or report
their cybersecurity: for the board, for customers, for an insurer or for a
regulator. No certificate is involved; the product gives you a clear,
defensible picture instead.

## What you get

| | |
|---|---|
| **The framework** | All 106 subcategories, in 22 categories and the 6 functions, GOVERN included |
| **Profile & Tiers** | Current and target Tiers for governance and for risk management |
| **Current and target profiles** | A sentence on each outcome for where you are now and where you intend to be, and the gap between them |
| **Guidance on every outcome** | What it means, what to do, what a reviewer looks for, and what proof to keep |
| **Get ready** | A plan of 7 stages and 24 steps, from knowing what you protect to acting on the gap. 18 steps tick themselves from your data |
| **Sample library** | 132 assets and 75 risks, the risks already pointing at the outcomes that deal with them |
| **Registers** | Evidence, risks, assets, policies, tasks, incidents and findings, all linked to the outcomes |
| **Reports (PDF, with your logo)** | Executive summary, gap report, readiness plan, risk register, evidence register |
| **Help and Documentation** | 10 pages in the product, including a CSF 2.0 playbook and where to find evidence in Microsoft 365, AWS and Google |

## Every Offset product also has

HTTPS with your certificate · four roles · every change recorded in an audit trail that auditors can read and download · nightly
backups and restore from the screen · signed one-click updates · email
reminders and a daily digest · Forgot password for administrators.

## How it installs

| | |
|---|---|
| **Windows** | One installer. Nothing else to install |
| **Linux** | A package that installs as a system service. Ubuntu, Debian, RHEL, Amazon Linux, SUSE |
| **Docker** | Pull the image and run it with Compose |
| **Needs** | A 64-bit server, about 300 MB of disk plus your evidence, and one port. It uses under 150 MB of memory at rest |
| **Sized for** | Up to about 50 users |

## Your data stays yours

It runs on your server, inside your network. No cloud, no telemetry, no
licence server. Offset Security has no access to it. Details are in the
security overview and the privacy sheet.
